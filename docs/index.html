What it checks

The misconfigurations attackers look for first

Most website breaches don't need a zero-day. They start with missing basics. SiteSentry finds them using only normal, browser-like requests.

Transport & TLS

HTTPS, HTTP→HTTPS redirects, certificate validity and expiry, TLS 1.3, legacy TLS 1.0/1.1, mixed content.

Browser defences

HSTS, Content-Security-Policy quality (unsafe-inline, wildcards, nonces), clickjacking, nosniff, referrer and permissions policies.

CORS trust

Sends a forged Origin to detect origin reflection with credentials, a high-impact data-theft bug.

Cookies

Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite flags, plus the invalid SameSite=None-without-Secure combination.

Email & DNS

SPF, DMARC and CAA records, queried with a DNS client written from scratch on raw UDP/TCP packets.

Leaks & exposure

Version banners, X-Powered-By fingerprints, security.txt, and an opt-in check for exposed Redis, MongoDB, RDP and more.

Not just a list of problems

Copy-paste fixes for your server

Every finding explains why it matters in plain English. SiteSentry then generates the exact config that fixes all of them at once.

  • nginx, Apache, Caddy and Express output, one click to copy
  • "Fix these first" list, sorted by real-world severity
  • Evidence and references (OWASP, MDN) for every check
  • HTML, PDF and JSON reports to share or automate
